================================================================
Right now a visitor meets three separate things with no spine:
  - 4 services that read like a MENU (pick one),
  - 3 brands that look like 3 UNRELATED companies,
  - a projects gallery that floats on its own.
Nothing answers the only question that matters: "What IS Novitatis, and why
are these things one company?" That is the fragmentation. It is not a design

the same UI suddenly reads as one system.

postcode), it is forward-leaning, and no peer in Part 2 of the research doc
claims it cleanly. Group NAO is the closest in spirit ("strategy & ideas

research, interviews, strategy for destinations and municipalities) plus the
three digital products. The think-tank credibility (research, innovation,
agenda-setting talks) is exactly what WINS the consulting and SELLS the

   -> Important nuance: do NOT drift into "pure think tank" copy that sounds

      and products are how it shows up and gets paid. Say both.

THE DIFFERENTIATOR: most tourism consultancies only advise (THR, Twenty31,
Toposophy all stop at the report). Novitatis researches, advises AND ships
the digital. Research and innovation, made real through digitalisation.

So everything hangs off a VALUE CHAIN, not a menu:

And the 3 BRANDS are not separate companies. They are the PRODUCTISED

That is the connective tissue. Say it once, clearly, and the brands stop

Projects / "Our Projects" = the PROOF the research-led method works.

C. THE INTERNATIONAL PIVOT — 5 concrete moves (my input)
================================================================
You want the MAIN brand (Novitatis) to read international. Hungary becomes
"where we proved it", not "who we serve". Five moves:

  1. LEAD WITH METHOD + OUTCOME, NOT GEOGRAPHY. "We turn research into
     strategy destinations act on" works in any country. No "Hungarian" in
     the hero.
  2. REFRAME HUNGARY AS PROOF OF RIGOUR. "Our methodology was adopted into
     national law" is evidence you operate at the highest level. Keep the
     decree in the PROOF layer (projects / sub-pages), not the hero.
  3. PUT THE INTERNATIONAL FOOTPRINT UP FRONT. ITB Berlin, Interreg Europe /
     Smart Tour (Côtes-d'Armor), Edinburgh. You already work across borders,
     so show it early as a credibility strip.
  4. STRIP LOCAL JARGON FROM PRIMARY COPY. NTAK, specific decree numbers,
     Hungarian org names -> move to proof/detail, not headlines. A German or
     French DMO should never hit a word they have to Google.
  5. CLIENT = "destinations and municipalities" (universal), never
     "Hungarian destinations". Register = Group NAO / DTTT international English.
