3. WHAT WE SPEAK ABOUT  (4 themes — the topics, grounded in real talks)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

THEME 01 — Being found in the age of AI search
   Search has changed. The customer no longer scrolls ten Google results, an AI
   answers for them. In 2026, 45% of consumers already use an AI tool to find a
   local business, up from 6% a year earlier. We explain GEO (Generative Engine
   Optimization, not SEO) in plain language, and what a destination or business
   has to do to stay visible.
   [talks: "Stay visible in the age of AI", DigiTurismo podcast, ITB Berlin]

THEME 02 — Digital visibility and the Google ecosystem
   94% of searches happen on Google platforms and nearly half are zero-click. If
   your provider journey breaks somewhere, the guest is gone. We show where it
   breaks, backed by an analysis of over 1,300 Lake Balaton provider websites.
   [talks: Tourism Summit 2025, National Tourinform Meeting, METU x CheckINN]

THEME 03 — Data-driven destination management
   How a destination runs on real data instead of guesswork. We take audiences
   inside Hungary's national tourism data system (NTAK/NTDSC): daily, automatic
   data from 95,000+ accommodations and AI forecasting, presented internationally
   as a European best practice.
   [talks: Smart Tour, Brittany (France); AI Opener, Edinburgh]

THEME 04 — Sustainability you can measure
   From 27 September 2026 the EU ECGT regulation requires measured evidence for
   "green" claims. We make the case simply: evidence is data, and data is
   digitalisation. Don't talk to the guest about sustainability, show them the
   numbers.
   [talks: Earth Day Conference, Smart Tour opening, Lake Balaton]
