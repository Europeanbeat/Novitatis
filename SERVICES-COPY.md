# Novitatis — Services content rewrite

Source voice: Miles Partnership (problem-solver, outcomes, "Let's work
together"), Destination Think (each service stands alone, research-led),
Tourism Tribe (practical, hands-on education). Proof: the brand booklet +
.agents/messaging.md + the references library.

Voice rules: British English, no em-dashes in prose, never "think tank",
evidence-led but warm. One ask everywhere: **Let's work together**.

---

## THE CORE FIX — 4 separate services, not one package

The problem (Vivi): the /services page reads like one connected, continuous
service. It should read as **four separate services** you can take on their own.
The fix is wording on three elements, the cards themselves are fine.

### 1. Showcase opener (services-showcase.tsx)
KEEP: heading "Accepting all challenges."
CHANGE sub-line to:
> "Four services, each one its own. Take the one your challenge needs, or
> combine them. No two challenges are the same, so neither is our response."

### 2. The tree heading (services-reactive-bg.tsx) — currently "Four services. One method."
This is the main offender ("One method" = one thing). Replace with:
> Heading: **"Four services. Four ways in."**
> Sub: "Consulting, development, education and public speaking each stand on
> their own, engage any single one. What they share is a research-led way of
> working, not a fixed package you have to buy whole."

### 3. The method spine (method-spine.tsx) — currently reads like one linear pipeline
Reframe from "every engagement follows the same sequence" to a *method we apply*,
not a package every project marches through:
> Heading: **"How we work on a challenge."**
> Sub: "Whichever service you pick, we work the same disciplined way: we frame
> the real question, gather the evidence, and build something you can measure.
> Not every project needs every step, we start where your challenge is."

## THE FOUR SERVICES — each standalone (sub-page leads)

Each sub-page opens by naming the CHALLENGE it solves (Miles style), then how we
work, then proof, then "Let's work together".

### 1. Consulting & strategy  (model: Destination Think)
- Lead: "Research and strategy you can defend, in front of a board, a ministry
  or a funder."
- DONE: "Research & analysis" showcase (resident sentiment + our real studies).
- Keep proof points (634/2020, bike route, HUF 1.45bn).

### 2. Development & digitalisation  (model: D3HUB)
- Lead: "Strategy turned into working digital systems and practical AI."
- Reframe around the **data-driven green & digital transition** for DMOs and
  tourism SMEs (the D3HUB idea Bence sent), our version:
  > "Tourism's digital transition is not one big leap, it is a series of
  > working systems. We help destinations and SMEs adopt data and AI that fit
  > how they actually operate: digital process design, pilots, and tools we
  > deliver through our own brands, Visible Tourism and ai4tourism."
- Proof: 1,300-provider study, VisitBalaton365 AI adoption, own brands at ITB.
- CTA: "Have a system or tool to build? Let's work together."

### 3. Education & mentoring  (model: Tourism Tribe)
- Header Bence wants: **"Empowering our industry."**
- Tourism Tribe voice (practical, outcomes, tailored):
  > "Bring your team's trickiest challenge and walk away with a plan they can
  > run themselves. We deliver national curricula, hands-on workshops with
  > handbooks, and one-to-one Google and AI mentoring under Turizmus Tudástár,
  > tailored to a region's needs and budget."
- Tie-in: the EU Pact for Skills (tourism skills) as the why-now.
- Proof: national programme since 2022, Dobosi/Keszthely workshops, Edinburgh/ITB.
- CTA: "Want to build capability in your team? Let's work together."

### 4. Public speaking  (model: Miles "industry engagement")
- DONE: "Industry engagement" beat (relationships + commitment to the sector).
- Keep the events/proof + CTA.
