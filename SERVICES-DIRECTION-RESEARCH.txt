PART 1 — THE STYLE YOU LIKE, DISTILLED INTO RULES
================================================================
Two references define the register: the Digital Tourism Think Tank (DTTT,
the digital/tourism authority voice) and Group NAO (the sharp, anti-buzzword
"what's next" voice). Together they give a clear house style:

1. QUESTION-FIRST, NOT ANSWER-FIRST.
   Group NAO: "We often question the question we have been asked."
   "The answers we find are no better than the questions we ask."
   -> Lead with the real problem, not your service menu.

2. ANTI-BUZZWORD, ANTI-THEATRE.
   Group NAO is "allergic to standardized strategy processes, empty
   buzzwords, and innovation theatre."
   -> No "synergy", "leverage", "holistic solutions". Plain and sharp wins.

3. SHORT, DECLARATIVE, SLIGHTLY PROVOCATIVE.
   DTTT: "Spot, Track and Compete." "Putting digital at the core."
   "Stay ahead of the curve." "Get that digital edge."
   -> 3-5 word headlines. Strong verbs. A little edge.

4. AUTHORITY THROUGH SPECIFICITY, NOT ADJECTIVES.
   DTTT: "Global Leaders on Digitalisation in Tourism" backed by a
   "proven methodology". THR: "1,100+ projects, 70+ countries."
   -> Earn authority with a number or a named outcome, not "leading" or
   "innovative". You have the strongest proof of anyone here: national law.

5. AGENDA-SETTING, FORWARD-LEANING.
   Group NAO: "navigate what's next." "agenda-setting reports."
   DTTT: "the importance of digital transformation", "think differently."
   -> Position Novitatis as shaping where tourism goes, not following it.
   This is exactly your public-speaking + AI angle.

6. OUTCOME OVER ACTIVITY.
   DTTT: "outcome-focused rather than theoretical." THR: "data-driven
   creative solutions to complex business challenges."
   -> Say what the client ends up with, not what you do all day.

HOUSE RULES TO KEEP (yours): British English. No em-dashes. Never
"result-based billing". Plain over clever. Benefit grounded in real proof.

PART 3 — RECOMMENDED POSITIONING (the one line)
================================================================
You already have a strong booklet line: "Not a classic consultancy.
A specialist network." Keep it. Sharpen the tourism + what's-next angle.

OPTIONS for the top-level positioning line (pick one to react to):
  P1. "A specialist network for what's next in tourism."
  P2. "We turn evidence into the direction tourism takes next."
  P3. "Research-led. Built for the digital future of tourism."
  P4. "Not a classic consultancy. A specialist network for tourism." (current)

My pick: P1 or P2. P1 borrows Group NAO's "what's next" energy and keeps
your "specialist network" identity. P2 is more declarative and ties your
research credibility (634/2020, 1,300 study) to a forward stance.
