# Novitatis Messaging Architecture (Message House)

*Built 2026-06-11 from `.agents/product-marketing.md`. Every page and every
section carries ONE slot from this house. If copy does not serve a slot, cut
it. British English, no em dashes in prose, no "result-based billing".*

---

## The Roof (core promise, everywhere)

**Research-led strategy for tourism. And the tools to deliver it.**

Long form: Novitatis turns evidence into strategy, working digital tools and
in-house capability, for destinations, public bodies and tourism businesses.

## The Three Pillars (reasons to believe)

### Pillar 1: Direction you can defend
Strategy built on field research and evidence, strong enough to take to a
board, a ministry or a funder.
- Proof: methodology adopted into national law (Decree 634/2020)
- Proof: Lake Balaton Bike Route feasibility + cost-benefit analysis
- Proof: HUF 1.45bn attraction investment supported, opened 2022
- Proof: brand and positioning strategy for tourism businesses (winery, SMEs)

### Pillar 2: Built, not just advised
The strategy becomes a working system: digital processes, AI adoption,
pilots, and our own products where they fit.
- Proof: 1,300 tourism providers researched, first study of its kind in Hungary
- Proof: AI adoption with the VisitBalaton365 DMO team
- Proof: own brands shipping: Visible Tourism, ai4tourism, Turizmus Tudástár

### Pillar 3: Capability that stays
Training, mentoring and agenda-setting so the knowledge remains in the
client's team when the engagement ends.
- Proof: national training programme built ground-up, running since 2022
- Proof: workshops with handbooks (Dobosi 2024, Keszthely 2025)
- Proof: international stage: ITB Berlin, Tourism Summit, Interreg, Edinburgh

## The Two Doors (audiences, named explicitly)

1. **Destinations & public bodies** — ministries, NTOs, DMOs/TDMs,
   municipalities. Promise: evidence and strategy that survive scrutiny,
   and the digital capability to act on them.
2. **Tourism businesses** — SMEs, accommodation, hospitality, attractions.
   Promise: the strategy, brand and online visibility work of a big
   consultancy, sized and priced for an SME.

## The Ask (single intent, everywhere)

"Tell us what you are working on." → contact. No competing CTAs.
