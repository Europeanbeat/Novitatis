## Product Overview
**One-liner:** Novitatis is a research-first consultancy and specialist network for the tourism sector, turning evidence into strategy, working solutions, capability, and influence.
**What it does:** Novitatis combines strategy-making with design-thinking research to help tourism organisations develop. Field work and scientifically grounded research come first; from there it produces strategies, feasibility studies, working pilots, training, and AI adoption that organisations actually act on. It is structured as an "expert network": a core team plus specialised external experts assembled per project.
**Product category:** Tourism development & innovation consultancy (strategy, digitalisation, education, thought leadership).
**Product type:** Professional services / consultancy (project-based engagements; also runs branded products — see Brands).

## Target Audience
**Target organisations (three tiers):**
1. Public & national — ministries, national tourism organisations (NTOs), regional development agencies.
2. Destination management — DMOs / TDMs (e.g. VisitBalaton365), municipalities.
3. Businesses & providers — tourism SMEs, accommodation, restaurants & hospitality, attractions & experiences.
**Decision-makers:** ministry/agency programme leads; DMO/TDM directors; municipality leadership; SME owners/managers.
**Primary use case:** "We need to develop our destination/organisation but lack the evidence, plan, digital capability, or in-house knowledge to do it credibly."
**Jobs to be done:**
- Produce a defensible strategy / feasibility case for a development or investment decision.
- Turn a validated idea into a working digital/service/organisational solution.
- Build lasting capability inside the team (training, mentoring, AI adoption).
- Make sense of where tourism is heading (digital, AI) and act on it.

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Tourism organisations invest in promotion and projects but rarely have evidence of what works; strategies read well then stall on contact with reality; outside help solves the immediate problem then leaves with the knowledge; and the sector changes faster than its rules and digital practice can keep up.
**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Classic consultancies deliver a document, not a working result, and don't transfer capability.
- Generic agencies lack tourism-sector and public-sector/regulatory depth.
- In-house teams lack the research method and bandwidth.
**What it costs them:** Wasted promotion budget, stalled/abandoned strategies, failed digital investment, and dependence on outside consultants.
**Emotional tension:** Pressure to show results to funders/leadership; fear of betting budget on the wrong direction; frustration that effort accumulates but evidence does not.

## Differentiation
**Key differentiators:**
- **Research-first / design-thinking method** — scientifically grounded research, field work, client involvement, and experimentation before any recommendation.
- **Expert-network model** — specialists assembled per project, not a fixed generalist team.
- **Tourism + public-sector depth** — work has been adopted into national law (Decree 634/2020).
- **Capability stays in-house** — training, mentoring, and AI adoption designed so the organisation can carry the work on.
- **Visual, decision-ready outputs** — graphic executive summaries (some picked up by the press).
- **Ahead on AI/GEO** — exhibiting and speaking on AI in tourism and visibility in the age of AI search.
**Why customers choose us:** evidence they can defend, work that actually ships, and knowledge that stays with them.
